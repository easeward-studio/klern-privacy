Privacy Policy for Savense

Effective date: 10 August 2026

Publisher and privacy contact

Savense is published by Easeward Studio. Privacy questions: savense.support@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

Savense stores transactions, categories, budgets, recurring entries and trips/wallets in local app storage on your device. Savense does not operate an account or cloud-sync service. Automatic Android cloud backup is disabled.

No analytics or tracking

This version of Savense contains no Firebase, Google Analytics, advertising, attribution, crash-reporting or other third-party analytics/tracking SDK. Easeward Studio does not receive app-usage analytics from Savense.

Exchange-rate service

For automatic currency conversion, Savense may request current or historical reference rates from the public Frankfurter API over HTTPS. Requests contain the source currency, target currency and, when relevant, a date. Savense does not include your transaction amount, note, category, budget or other financial records in the rate request.

Exports and backups

Backup, CSV and Excel files are created only when you request them and are saved to the destination you choose. Savense does not upload those files to Easeward Studio.

Biometrics and permissions

Savense may use Android biometric or device-credential authentication. Biometric templates are handled by Android and are not available to Savense. Internet access is used for optional exchange-rate requests. Savense does not request contacts, microphone, camera, precise location or photo-library permissions.

Contacting support

Contact Support opens your email app with a draft containing app version/build, Android version, device manufacturer/model and selected app language. You can review or edit it before sending. If you send it, Easeward Studio receives your email address, message and any information you leave in the draft.

Retention and deletion

Your financial records remain on your device until you delete them. You can delete individual records or reset records under More → Data & Backup. Uninstalling Savense removes app-local data subject to Android behavior. Exported files must be deleted separately.

Advertising

This version contains no advertising SDK and shows no ads. If advertising or analytics is introduced later, this policy and any legally required consent choices will be updated before related data is collected.

Your rights

Your financial records are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to Easeward Studio. You can manage or delete them directly in Savense. If you contact support, applicable data-protection law may give you rights regarding personal data contained in that communication, including access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection where relevant.

Changes

We may update this policy when Savense's features or data practices change. The effective date will be updated when material changes are made.
